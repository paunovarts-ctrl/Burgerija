Poreč's original burger bar

It started with a simple idea on the Adriatic coast: a real burger, made by hand, served where the sea meets the sunset. Burgerija became the first burger bar in Poreč, and quickly the place locals and travellers return to, season after season.

Everything is built fresh. Patties pressed by hand, buns baked daily, sauces made in-house, and ingredients sourced locally across Istria. Nothing frozen, nothing shortcut. Just fire, craft, and a table by the water.
