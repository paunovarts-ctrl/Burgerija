The first in Poreč

Burgerija opened as the town's first burger bar, right on the seafront promenade. Locals came first, then the travellers, and both keep coming back season after season.

Everything is built fresh. Patties pressed by hand, buns baked daily, sauces made in-house, and ingredients sourced locally across Istria. Nothing frozen, nothing shortcut. Just fire, craft, and a table by the water.
